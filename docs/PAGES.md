# SingleShelf Pages

## Public / Shared
- **Homepage** — Hero, recent activity, price highlights, daily play teaser, trending.
- **Games** — Searchable list of all games on the site (filters by platform, genre, etc.).
- **Game Page** — Individual game: cover, completion times, ratings (paid aggregate), reviews (paid full view), price history, wishlist button.
- **Prices** — Current deals, price tracking, sale alerts.
- **Play** — Daily minigames (paid access).
- **Community** — Forums (paid only).

## User-specific
- **My Shelf** — Personal collection: beaten, backlog, wishlist, completion times.
- **Account** — Profile, subscription status, settings, payment.
